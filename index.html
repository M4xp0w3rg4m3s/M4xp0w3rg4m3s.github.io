Giving players freedom, and making it feel good

What drives me to make games is the chance to make a player's day a little happier, whether through total freedom to approach a problem or through one highly satisfying mechanic. Playing games like Baldur's Gate 3 showed me how far player agency can go when an environment supports endless ways to be completed.

On our class project The Fable of the Eclipse I designed core gameplay loops and puzzle mechanics, then joined the programming team to script them myself. I enjoy the full cycle: building a feature, testing it with real people, reading the results and tweaking variables until it plays tight.

My long-term goal is to grow into a managing role, as I love being connected to my team, but right now I want to learn from senior developers and contribute wherever I add the most value.
